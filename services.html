What We Offer

Services

From a single restored window pane to a full architectural installation — every project starts with a conversation about your space.

Custom Commissions

Bespoke stained glass windows and panels designed around your space, light, and color preferences. You choose the palette — we make it hold light.

Restoration & Repair

Careful repair of antique and damaged stained glass, matching original techniques and materials. The character stays. The cracks don't.

Glass Sculptures

One-of-a-kind freestanding sculptures for homes, gardens, and public spaces. Built to be seen from every angle.

Glass Fusion Art

Kiln-formed bowls, panels, and wall pieces using layered fused glass techniques. Heat and color, shaped by hand.

Architectural Installations

Large-scale work for churches, businesses, and residential projects, including on-site consultation. From a castle's windows to your own front door.

Gifts & Small Pieces

Smaller sun-catchers, ornaments, and decorative pieces available to order. Small enough to gift, still entirely handmade.

How a commission works

1. Consultation

We talk through your space, style, budget, and timeline — no detail is too small to ask about.

2. Design & Approval

A custom design and material sample are prepared for your sign-off.

3. Crafting & Install

Each piece is handcrafted in the studio, then delivered or installed on-site.
