The Artist

About

Blanka Klaić is a Croatian stained glass and glass fusion artist, working on commissions ranging from church windows to public memorial sculpture.

Blanka Klaić

For Blanka Klaić, glass has always been more than just a material. It has been part of her life for as long as she can remember.

Growing up surrounded by glass, she was introduced to its possibilities at an early age through her father, who owned a glass company specializing in glass production and processing, established in the 1950s. Watching him work, learning about the material, and discovering the many ways it could be shaped and transformed naturally sparked her curiosity.

Among all the techniques, stained glass — and in particular the Tiffany technique — captured her imagination. Fascinated by the way individual pieces of colored glass could come together to create something extraordinary, she began attending courses, developing her skills through hands-on experience, and learning through observation and practice.

What started as a childhood fascination gradually grew into a lifelong passion and, eventually, her creative profession.

Today, Blanka continues to explore the endless possibilities of glass through her stained glass art and fused glass creations. Alongside traditional stained glass, she has expanded her expertise into glass fusing, creating unique bowls, jewelry, decorative objects, and one-of-a-kind pieces that combine color, texture, light, and form.

Her work ranges from ambitious architectural projects for churches and castles — including a full restoration of the stained glass windows at Trakošćan Castle, and a glass-and-steel memorial sculpture honoring fallen Croatian defenders of the Homeland War — to bespoke stained glass commissions for private clients. Whether restoring or creating an impressive architectural feature, or designing a personal piece for someone's home, she approaches every project with the same dedication to craftsmanship, detail, and artistic expression.

Sharing her knowledge is another important part of her work. Blanka regularly holds workshops in both stained glass and fused glass, inviting others to discover the beauty of working with glass, learn traditional techniques, and experience the joy of creating something with their own hands.

For Blanka, glass remains a material full of surprises and possibilities — a lifelong fascination that continues to inspire her to create, experiment, and bring beautiful ideas to life.
