Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Vitraž located?

Vitraž is based in Croatia.

Does Vitraž ship stained glass and glass art across Europe?

Yes. While the studio is based in Croatia, commissions are designed, crafted, and shipped to clients across Europe.

Can I commission a custom stained glass window or sculpture?

Yes. Every piece can be designed around your space, palette, and story — see the Services page to get started.

Does Vitraž offer stained glass workshops?

Yes. Hands-on stained glass and glass fusion workshops are available for beginners and returning students — see the Workshops page for dates.
