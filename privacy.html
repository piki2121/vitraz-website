Good to Know

Privacy Policy

Vitraž is a small, independent stained glass and glass fusion studio run by Blanka Klaić. This page explains what information this website collects and how it's used.

Who this is

This website, vitraz.eu, is run by Blanka Klaić, operating as Vitraž, based in Croatia. For any question about this policy or your data, contact vitraz.eu@gmail.com.

Contact form

When you send a message through the Contact page, the name, email address, and message you provide are sent to Vitraž via Formspree, a third-party form service, and arrive by email. That information is used only to reply to your enquiry - about a commission, workshop, or general question - and isn't used for any other purpose or shared with anyone else.

Cookies and analytics

On your first visit, a small banner asks whether you're OK with analytics cookies. If you accept, this site uses Google Analytics to understand which pages are visited and how people generally find the site - this is anonymised, aggregate information, not anything that identifies you personally. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set. You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser's site data for vitraz.eu, which will show the banner again.

Separately, this site uses your browser's local storage (not a tracking cookie) to remember two harmless preferences: your choice of light or dark theme, and whether you prefer the English or Croatian version. This stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Fonts

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Like any external resource, this means your browser makes a request to Google's servers, which involves sharing your IP address with Google.

Other services linked from this site

Links to Instagram and (once live) Etsy take you to those companies' own websites, which have their own privacy practices, separate from this one.

What's not collected

This site doesn't ask for or store any payment information, and it isn't used to track you across other websites.

Your rights

As a visitor based in the EU, you can ask what information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or simply ask a question about any of this - email vitraz.eu@gmail.com and it'll be sorted out directly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page. Last updated: November 2026.
