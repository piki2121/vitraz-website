Frequently Asked Questions

Vitraž creates handcrafted stained glass and glass fusion art, teaches hands-on workshops in both techniques, and accepts bespoke commissions. Here's what people most often ask about each.

Custom Glass Creations & Commissions

Can I order custom-made fused glass artwork?

Absolutely! We create bespoke fused glass pieces based on individual ideas, preferences and requirements.

From handmade plates and serving sets to decorative trays, ashtrays and unique interior objects, we can turn your idea into a one-of-a-kind glass creation.

Whether you're looking for something functional, decorative or a special personalised gift, get in touch to discuss your project.

Do you create seasonal and special-edition glass pieces?

Yes! Alongside our regular fused glass creations, we also produce special and seasonal collections.

One example is our handmade glass Christmas ornaments, created as unique decorative pieces and thoughtful gifts.

For available creations, small gifts and selected fused glass artworks, you can also visit our Etsy shop.

Can I order custom stained glass windows for my home or business?

Yes! We accept private commissions for bespoke stained glass creations, including windows and doors.

Each project is developed according to the client's ideas, architectural setting and personal preferences. Whether you have a clear design in mind or need help developing an idea, we'd be happy to discuss the possibilities.

Do you work on larger architectural stained glass projects?

Yes. Our work extends beyond individual decorative pieces to larger architectural commissions.

We undertake stained glass projects for churches, castles and other architectural spaces, as well as private residential and commercial projects. Each commission is approached individually, with attention to the character of the space, the design and the technical requirements of the project.

Can you create special glass sculptures, trophies or unique objects?

Yes! We love exploring the possibilities of glass beyond traditional applications.

In addition to stained glass windows and fused glass objects, we accept special commissions for glass sculptures, trophies and other unique creations. If you have an idea that you'd like to see brought to life in glass, we'd love to hear about it.

Where can I see and buy your fused glass creations?

You can explore a selection of our handmade fused glass artworks, decorative objects and small gifts in our Etsy shop.

For bespoke pieces, larger commissions or something you have imagined but cannot find in our existing collection, please contact us directly.

What if I have an idea for something made of glass that isn't listed here?

We'd love to hear about it!

Glass is an incredibly versatile material, and we're always interested in exploring new ideas, techniques and creative possibilities. Whether it's a unique decorative object, a special gift, an architectural feature or an entirely unconventional concept, don't hesitate to get in touch.

Tell us what you have in mind, and let's explore how we can bring your idea to life in glass.

Stained Glass Workshops

How can I attend a stained glass workshop?

There are several ways to join one of our stained glass workshops. You can sign up for one of our scheduled workshops, which we regularly announce on our Instagram profile. Follow us to stay up to date with upcoming dates, locations and registration details.

Alternatively, you can organise a private workshop for your own group. Whether you're planning a creative afternoon with friends, a couple's experience, a team-building activity or simply want to try something new, we'd be happy to create a workshop tailored to your group.

Can I organise a private stained glass workshop?

Absolutely! Private workshops can be organised for groups of friends, couples, families, colleagues or any group interested in exploring the art of stained glass.

We welcome groups of up to 8 participants per workshop. Keeping the group small allows us to give everyone individual attention, guide them through each step and ensure a rewarding learning experience.

We can also organise workshops for mixed groups of people who don't necessarily know each other – sometimes the best creative connections happen that way!

Do I need any previous experience to join a stained glass workshop?

Not at all! Our workshops are suitable for complete beginners as well as those who already have some experience working with glass.

You'll be guided through the entire process, from understanding the material and choosing your design to learning how to handle, cut, grind and assemble glass. No previous artistic or technical knowledge is required – just curiosity and a willingness to create.

What will I learn during a stained glass workshop?

You'll discover the fascinating world of stained glass and learn about glass as a material, its characteristics and the techniques used to transform it into art.

Depending on the workshop, you'll explore glass selection, cutting, grinding, shaping and assembling individual pieces. We'll guide you through the process step by step, sharing practical knowledge and techniques along the way.

Most importantly, you'll experience the satisfaction of creating something beautiful with your own hands.

Do I need to bring my own materials or tools?

No. All materials, tools and equipment needed for the workshop are provided. You simply need to bring yourself and your creativity!

Can I choose what I want to create during the stained glass workshop?

Yes! We offer different workshop formats depending on your preferences and experience.

Some workshops follow a selection of prepared designs and motifs, allowing you to focus on learning the techniques. For private workshops, you can also choose your own design and create something more personal, with our guidance to help bring your idea to life.

Where do stained glass workshops take place? Do you travel?

One of the best things about our stained glass workshops is that we can bring the experience to you!

Unlike many traditional workshops, you don't have to travel to our studio. We are happy to organise and host stained glass workshops in different locations throughout Croatia and, depending on the group size and duration of the workshop, beyond Croatia as well.

Travel arrangements are discussed individually, based on the location, number of participants and workshop duration.

Are stained glass workshops suitable for team building?

Definitely! Working with glass is a wonderful way to step away from everyday routines, slow down and reconnect with your creative side.

Our workshops are ideal for corporate team-building events, groups of friends, couples, celebrations and creative gatherings. They can be organised exclusively for your group or as part of a mixed workshop with other participants.

The process encourages focus, patience and experimentation, while offering the pleasure of making something unique. It's a creative experience that brings people together and leaves everyone with something tangible to remember it by.

Glass Fusing Workshops

What is a glass fusing workshop?

Glass fusing is a technique in which pieces of glass are arranged into a composition and then heated in a specialised kiln until they bond together, creating a new object or artwork.

During our glass fusing workshops, you'll have the opportunity to experiment with colours, shapes, textures and different glass elements to create your own unique piece. It's an exciting process that combines creativity with the fascinating transformation of glass through heat.

What can I make during a glass fusing workshop?

The possibilities are almost endless! Depending on your ideas and the selected format, you can create functional objects or decorative artworks, including:

  • Bowls and plates
  • Mirrors
  • Vases
  • Jewellery
  • Lamp shades
  • Candle holders
  • Serving trays
  • Decorative and abstract art pieces

You can follow your own inspiration and design something personal, with our guidance throughout the creative process.

Can I create my own design during a glass fusing workshop?

Of course! One of the most exciting parts of working with fused glass is the freedom to experiment.

You can come with your own idea, choose your colours and shapes, and create a completely individual design. We'll help you understand how different glass elements work together and guide you towards the best possible result.

Do I need any previous experience to attend a glass fusing workshop?

Not at all. Our glass fusing workshops are designed for beginners and curious creatives alike. We'll introduce you to the material, explain the process and guide you through creating your own artwork.

All materials and equipment needed during the workshop are provided.

Where do glass fusing workshops take place?

Unlike stained glass workshops, glass fusing workshops take place at our location. This is because the specialised kilns and machinery required for glass fusing cannot be transported.

We keep our groups small, with a maximum of 8 participants, to ensure everyone receives individual guidance and has enough space to explore their creativity.

Can I take my fused glass creation home on the same day?

Not quite! Fused glass requires a specialised kiln-firing process, which takes time and cannot be rushed.

After the workshop, your creations are carefully fired in the kiln. You'll be able to see the finished result the following day, once the firing and cooling process is complete.

This is part of the magic of glass fusing – the final transformation is revealed only after the glass has been through the kiln.

Can I organise a private glass fusing workshop?

Yes! You can join one of our scheduled workshops or arrange a private session for your own group.

Whether you're planning a creative afternoon with friends, a special experience for two, a celebration or a team-building activity, we'll help you organise a workshop that suits your needs.

Private workshops are limited to 8 participants to ensure an individual and personal approach.
