Learn the Craft

Stained Glass Workshops

Learn to cut, grind and assemble glass using the copper-foil technique, and leave with a finished piece of your own. Suitable for complete beginners through to returning students working on something more ambitious.

Scheduled workshops are announced on Instagram, or you can book a private session for your own group — a creative afternoon with friends, a couple's experience, a celebration, or a team-building event. Private sessions welcome up to 8 participants, and we're happy to travel to a location of your choice across Croatia.

Learn the Craft

Glass Fusing Workshops

Arrange colour, shape and texture into your own composition, then let the kiln do the rest. Glass fusing workshops are a hands-on introduction to kiln-forming — no previous experience needed.

Because the kilns can't travel, these sessions are held at our studio, with small groups of up to 8. Private sessions can also be arranged — for friends, a special occasion, or a one-on-one experience. Pieces are fired overnight, so you'll see the finished result the following day.
